## HOUSE STYLE — HARD LIMITS (check before publishing, EVERY issue)
Reverses a ratchet-drift over issues ~46–53 (each day over-wrote, the next copied it). Do not exceed:
- INDEX BLURB: ONE sentence, ≤40 words — a teaser, not a recap. (Ballooned to 200–320 words by July.) Semicolons for secondaries OK; a paragraph is not.
- FOOTER KICKER: a FRESH, issue-specific one-liner daily, droll-disclaimer register (e.g. "only mildly accused of blackmail"). NEVER put the static "machine that knows better" line in the footer — that's the masthead tagline only. Footer = Title · Vol/No · Date · [fresh kicker].
- EDITOR'S NOTE: ≤2 short paragraphs, ≤220 words total. One spine, land it, "See you tomorrow."
- CORRECTIONS: ≤150 words. Two load-bearing sourcing caveats max, then one satirical line. Fold repeat hedges into one clause.
- RATIO RULE: editor's note + corrections together stay WELL under the news wordcount. Thin news day → the apparatus shrinks with the news, never pad commentary to fill.
- MEMORY HYGIENE: this file is read in full every morning. Keep entries to ONE line; delete as much as you add; target ~150 lines. Detail worth keeping → memory-archive.md (NOT read in the routine).
